\section{Can Either Premise Be Checked From Published Papers?}
\label{sec:bg-survey}

The two premises can be checked from a paper only if it reports the per-system scores behind its pooled comparisons, with enough released material to attach an uncertainty to them, and states how its scores were produced.
To see how often this holds, we coded 26 microservice RCA method and benchmark papers from 2021 to 2026, a convenience sample, with two independent coders working from a codebook; the selection, codebook, coding, and agreement are in the replication package.
Scope is partly visible: \SurveyQOneYes of the 26 papers report a score per system ($\kappa = \SurveyKappaQOne$), so a reader can see where a pooled lead does not hold, but not with what uncertainty.
Checking the repositories and archives linked from each paper on 2 October 2026, we found \SurveyRelAny papers that release per-case outputs, each for one method or one setting only: the predictions of \openrca's agent with one model~\citep{xu2025openrca} and the rankings of the authors' own method in Nezha~\citep{yu2023nezha} and CausalRCA~\citep{causalrca}; for \SurveyRelFullUndet papers the linked release had expired or could not be listed.
None of the \SurveyRelFullCheckable papers whose releases we could check releases per-case outputs for all methods of its main comparison, so for those comparisons a reader cannot compute a paired difference, its uncertainty, or a coverage figure.
Provenance is less visible still: \SurveyQFourYes papers state which input representation or preprocessing they used and \SurveyQFourNotYes do not, with moderate agreement between coders ($\kappa = \SurveyKappaQFour$).
Superiority claims are nonetheless stated over the benchmark as a whole, for example ``The top-1 accuracy of TraceRCA outperforms the state-of-the-art unsupervised approaches by 44.8\%''~\citep{li2021tracerca} and ``Extensive experiments conducted on the synthetic and realworld microservice-based datasets demonstrate that RUN noticeably outperforms the state-of-the-art root cause analysis methods''~\citep{lin2024run}.
