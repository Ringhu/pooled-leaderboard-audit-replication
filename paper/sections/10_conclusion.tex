\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A pooled leaderboard reports one number per method and leaves unstated on which systems the advantage holds and how the score was produced.
In three RCA benchmark families, whether a pooled lead between published methods held on every subsystem was decided by a quantity the leaderboard withholds, the spread of the lead across subsystems: leads whose margin exceeded it held everywhere, most leads below it reversed, and every wrong choice on a held-out subsystem was of the second kind.
On a second release of the same applications, one method's response to newly recorded metric kinds accounted for five of the six comparisons that changed sign, and the input table, the handling of failed outputs, and the denominator of the released pipelines changed conclusions without raising an error.
Stating the subsystems and the spread next to every pooled comparison, naming the input table and the denominator, and releasing the per-case outputs behind it would let readers check what the leaderboard does not say.

\section*{Data Availability}
An anonymous replication package is available to the reviewers at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/pooled-leaderboard-audit-replication-8DB8/}.
It contains the frozen per-case table with its SHA-256 digest, the run ledger, the run and analysis scripts, the adapted \simplerca code, the integrity-check diagnostics, the survey data, and additional result tables; a quick check regenerates every number, table, and figure of the paper from the per-case table in a few minutes, and a full rerun starts from the public benchmark data, which the package does not redistribute.
We will make the package public upon acceptance.
