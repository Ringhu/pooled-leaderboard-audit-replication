\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A pooled leaderboard reports one number per method and leaves unstated on which systems the advantage holds and how the score was produced.
In three RCA benchmark families, published methods reversed on individual subsystems, on a second release of the same applications, and on held-out subsystems, and the input table, the handling of failed outputs, and the denominator of the released pipelines changed conclusions without raising an error.
Stating both premises next to every pooled comparison, and releasing the per-case outputs behind it, would let readers check what the leaderboard does not say.
