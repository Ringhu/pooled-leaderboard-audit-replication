\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Benchmark validity in machine learning.}
Pooled leaderboards have been criticized for hiding condition-specific behavior and for depending on which benchmarks are included~\citep{dehghani2021benchmark,bowman2021will,liang2022helm}.
Test-set replication and label errors can reorder methods~\citep{recht2019imagenet,northcutt2021pervasive}, and hidden stratification and underspecification describe models whose average performance hides failures on subgroups~\citep{oakdenrayner2020hidden,damour2022underspecification,sagawa2020distributionally}., and checklists for benchmark quality ask for documentation and reproducible releases~\citep{betterbench,pineau2021improving}.
We apply this view to RCA benchmarks, where subsystems are identifiable applications, per-case scores are available, and the release itself can be under-specified.

\textbf{Empirical software engineering.}
Guidelines for empirical research in software engineering ask that a study state its population, treatments, and analysis in enough detail to be repeated~\citep{kitchenham2002guidelines}, and catalogues of recurring problems in software analytics papers include inadequate reporting of data preparation and conclusions whose stability is never tested~\citep{menzies2019badsmells}.
In defect prediction, the research group explained more of the variation in reported performance than the classifier did~\citep{shepperd2014researcher}; cross-project experiments found that whether a predictor transfers depends on the projects involved~\citep{zimmermann2009crossproject}, a benchmark of cross-project approaches found their ranking to depend on the datasets and metrics used~\citep{herbold2018comparative}, and the model-validation technique was shown to change performance estimates and rankings~\citep{tantithamthavorn2017validation}.
We ask the same questions of RCA benchmarks: whether a comparison holds on each evaluated system, and which part of a score the evaluation procedure produced.

\textbf{RCA benchmarks and methods.}
Offline RCA benchmarks include \rcaeval~\citep{pham2024rcaeval}, \openrca~\citep{xu2025openrca}, \petshop~\citep{hardt2024petshop}, and LEMMA-RCA~\citep{zheng2024lemma}; AIOpsLab and ITBench evaluate agents against live systems, and Cloud-OpsBench against recorded state snapshots replayed through diagnostic tools~\citep{aiopslab,jha2025itbench,wang2026cloudopsbench}.
Audited methods span change-point detection (\baro~\citep{pham2024baro}), causal inference and discovery (\circa~\citep{li2022circa}, \rcd~\citep{ikram2022rcd}, \causalrca~\citep{causalrca}, \cfa~\citep{budhathoki2022causal}), anomaly scoring (\ediag~\citep{ediagnosis}), and trace analysis (\tracerca~\citep{li2021tracerca}, \microrank~\citep{yu2021microrank}).

\textbf{Evaluating RCA evaluation.}
\citet{pham2024howfar} compare causal-inference RCA methods on common data and find that no method stands out in all situations. We do not re-establish that winners differ; we ask, for the pairwise comparisons a released benchmark supports, on which subsystems a pooled lead holds and which part of it the evaluation pipeline produced, with matched per-case outputs that others can recheck.
\citet{fang2025simplerca} question benchmark difficulty (\cref{sec:disc-fang}).
Studies of LLM agents for cloud RCA analyze why agents fail~\citep{kim2026agentfail,riddell2026stalled}; they concern a different evaluation mode from ours.
